# Source Corpus Quality

The quality auditor applies ten fail-closed gates to proposed source-corpus
review packages: source integrity, evidence completeness, unsupported inference,
hierarchy consistency, cross-course contamination, procedure evidence, mapping
evidence, conflict completeness, coverage, and rights/provenance.

It rejects source-free nodes and mappings, broken locations, unsupported
inferences, silent conflict removal, cross-course mappings, and any attempted
canonical authority. Its outputs are review-routing actions only; they never
write canonical records, databases, adaptive-platform state, or student-visible
content.
