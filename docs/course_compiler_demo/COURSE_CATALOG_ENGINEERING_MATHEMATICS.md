# Engineering Mathematics Course Catalog

This deterministic catalog adds Numerical Methods, Engineering Analysis, and Applied Mathematics. Every course is explicitly noncanonical and human-review-required, with 8 units, 25 topics, 50 micro-skills, 15 procedures, 15 generation families, 49 prerequisite relationships, two assessment blueprints, and a target production count of 300.

## Answer-engine allocation

Each course allocates five representations: `numeric_scalar`, `symbolic_expression`, `matrix`, `graph_diagram`, and `scientific_structured_response`. The 15 families rotate evenly across them. Each family declares its micro-skill, procedure, bounded parameter domains, difficulty allocation, engine-specific answer contract, failure signals, combined practice/summative role, and a deterministic parameter fingerprint with zero permitted exact duplicates.

The baseline registry can execute the numeric families. The other four representations remain explicit disabled-engine dependencies until the corresponding answer-engine lanes are synthesized. They are never downgraded to numeric or multiple-choice grading, and every blocked planned job retains its structured blocker.

## Curriculum and assessment policy

Procedures require declared variables, assumptions, dimensions, mathematical representation, and an independent residual or limiting-behavior check. Failure allocations cover model selection, algebra, convergence, dimensions, representation, and unsupported assumptions. Optional assets are limited to coordinate graphs, matrix tables, and engineering schematics with rights evidence; student-performance data is forbidden.

Both assessment blueprints cover all units and declared prerequisites and distribute work across numeric, symbolic, matrix, graph, and structured-response questions. The packs define proposed curriculum and deterministic generation capacity only. They do not contain validated production questions, grant canonical authority, perform activation, or infer unrestricted symbolic/prose grading.

Validation proves all common pack gates, hierarchy and procedure coverage, prerequisite resolution, exact five-engine allocation, family contract completeness, deterministic serialization, 300 unique planned jobs per course, explicit disabled-engine blockers, and fail-closed mutation behavior.
