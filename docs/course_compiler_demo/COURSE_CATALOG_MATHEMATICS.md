# Remaining Mathematics Course Catalog

Lane 044G adds nine deterministic, noncanonical, human-review-required course packs: Pre-Algebra, Algebra II, Geometry, Trigonometry, Pre-Calculus, Calculus II, Calculus III, Differential Equations, and Linear Algebra. The existing Algebra I and Calculus I builder is deliberately unchanged, including its deterministic evidence hash.

Each course declares its STEM/mathematics identity, course prerequisites, eight sequenced units, 25 linked topics, 50 linked micro-skills, 15 procedures, 15 generation families, a 49-edge internal prerequisite graph, a three-level difficulty model, answer-engine and failure-signal allocations, a rights-aware asset policy, two assessment blueprints, and a 300-question production target.

Generation families explicitly bind one micro-skill and procedure to bounded parameter domains, a normalized and independently derived answer contract, difficulty allocation, an answer engine, failure signals, an assessment role, and semantic/parameter duplicate controls. Engine references cover numeric, multiple-choice, symbolic, equation-system, coordinate-graph, and matrix capabilities; catalog references do not themselves activate an engine.

## Authority and validation boundary

The catalog makes no canonical claim and cannot promote or activate curriculum. Every pack and course remains `noncanonical=true` and `human_review_required=true`. Validation fails closed on missing courses, insufficient coverage, duplicate or unresolved identities, broken hierarchy/prerequisites, incomplete procedures, unsatisfied generation-family fields, unknown engine or failure allocations, malformed probability distributions, invalid parameter ranges, incomplete assets or blueprints, incorrect production targets, and integrity-hash drift.

## Independent catalog audit

The audit checks the common pack gates course-by-course, cross-reference closure, deterministic serialization, mutation isolation, and preservation of the legacy Algebra I/Calculus I hash. It also adversarially mutates authority flags, identities, hierarchy links, procedure coverage, prerequisite links, allocations, answer contracts, parameters, duplicate controls, failure signals, asset policy, blueprint coverage/distributions, target counts, and the integrity hash. No database, canonical store, production bank, or student-visible system is read or written by the catalog.
