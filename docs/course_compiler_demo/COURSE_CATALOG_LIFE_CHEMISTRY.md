# Life-Science and Advanced Chemistry Catalog

Lane 044K adds deterministic, noncanonical, human-review-required packs for Biology, Organic Chemistry, and Biochemistry. The established General Chemistry builder remains unchanged, including its evidence hash.

Every new course contains its STEM domain and subject identity, course prerequisites, eight units, 25 topics, 50 micro-skills, 15 procedures, 15 fully bound generation families, internal prerequisite relationships, a three-level difficulty model, answer-engine and failure-signal allocations, a rights-aware asset policy, two assessment blueprints, and a production target of 300 questions.

Allocations use scientific structured responses, bounded rubrics, numeric answers, and multiple choice. Organic Chemistry and Biochemistry additionally use machine-readable chemical formulas and declared chemical reactions. Structured responses require declared concepts and contradiction checks. Reaction contracts require atom/charge balance and a declared reaction; they do not accept unrestricted mechanism narratives.

## Explicit unsupported boundary

The catalog does not claim molecular-drawing grading, unrestricted mechanism grading, freeform essay grading, OCR, or image-based structure interpretation. Molecular drawing and image interpretation are explicitly disabled in each course asset policy. These course packs only allocate engine contracts; they do not activate engines or silently substitute another answer type.

## Validation and independent audit

Validation fails closed on canonical claims, missing unsupported-capability declarations, unresolved course or internal prerequisites, incomplete coverage, duplicate identities, hierarchy drift, incomplete procedure coverage, cross-procedure family bindings, unsupported engines, unbounded structured/reaction contracts, malformed parameters or difficulty distributions, unknown failure signals, missing duplicate controls, insufficient assets or blueprints, incorrect production targets, and integrity-hash drift. The independent audit also verifies preservation of General Chemistry and adversarially tests overclaim boundaries after recomputing the catalog hash.
